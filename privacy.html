Governance & Confidentiality

Client Confidentiality & Privacy Charter

Nexora Advisory treats all prospective client briefs, financial disclosures, and operational parameters with strict institutional discretion.

1. Non-Disclosure Commitment

Information submitted through our digital portal or shared during partner consultations is subject to strict professional non-disclosure obligations. Mutual NDAs are executed prior to any on-site discovery or codebase inspection.

2. Digital Security & Secret Isolation

All client inquiry transmissions utilize TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. In compliance with ScaleNova security architecture, zero client credentials or database secrets are exposed in client-side code.

3. Data Sovereignty & Indian DPDP Act Compliance

All client data is stored on sovereign Indian infrastructure, adhering to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
