Unlike traditional mega-consultancies where senior partners disappear after the pitch, every Nexora mandate is directly staffed and executed by a named Practice Director with a contracted minimum hours commitment.

Diagnostic sprints are fixed-price to ensure complete neutrality. Retained transformation partnerships combine a predictable quarterly retainer with mutually agreed performance milestones tied to working capital release or EBITDA margin goals.
